Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  We take your privacy seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using SugarMe, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SugarMe, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you sign up
  • Chat data — your conversations with AI characters, stored to enable chat history
  • Usage data — message counts, photo requests, and daily usage for plan enforcement
  • Payment data — processed securely by Razorpay; we do not store your card details
  • Device information — browser type, device type, and IP address for security purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and improve the SugarMe platform
  • Save and restore your chat history
  • Enforce daily usage limits based on your subscription plan
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send important account-related emails (no spam)
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Data Storage

Your account data and chat history are stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service provided by Google. Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest.

Your data is stored on Google's servers, which comply with international data protection standards.

4. Third-Party Services

SugarMe uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and account management
  • Razorpay — secure payment processing for subscriptions
  • Groq AI — AI language model for generating chat responses
  • Vercel — website hosting and deployment

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Chat Privacy

Your conversations with AI characters are private to your account. We do not share your chat history with other users or third parties. Chat data is used solely to provide you with continuous chat history across sessions.

SugarMe staff may access chat data only when required for technical support or to investigate reports of abuse.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and chat history for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@sugarme.in.

8. Cookies

SugarMe uses minimal cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

SugarMe is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover that a user is underage, we will immediately delete their account and associated data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of SugarMe after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Email: support@sugarme.in
  • Website: sugarme.vercel.app
