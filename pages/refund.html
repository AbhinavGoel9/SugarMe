Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  We want you to be happy with SugarMe.

We understand that sometimes things don't go as expected. This Refund Policy outlines when you are eligible for a refund and how to request one. Please read it carefully before making a purchase.

1. Our Refund Window

SugarMe offers a 24-hour refund window from the time of purchase. If you are not satisfied with your subscription, you may request a full refund within 24 hours of payment.

After 24 hours, we are unable to process refunds for subscription payments.

2. Eligible Refund Situations

✓ You MAY be eligible for a refund if:

  • You request a refund within 24 hours of purchase
  • You were charged twice for the same subscription due to a technical error
  • You were charged the wrong amount due to a pricing error on our end
  • The platform was completely unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours due to our error
  • You did not receive the subscription benefits you paid for due to a technical issue

✗ You are NOT eligible for a refund if:

  • More than 24 hours have passed since your purchase
  • You have used the subscription features (messages, photo requests, character access)
  • You forgot to cancel before the renewal date
  • You simply changed your mind after using the service
  • Your account was suspended for violating our Terms of Service
  • You request a partial refund for unused days in a billing period

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  • Email us at support@sugarme.in within 24 hours of your purchase
  • Use the subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Email]"
  • Include your registered email address and the date of purchase
  • Briefly explain why you are requesting a refund

We will respond to your refund request within 2 business days. Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

4. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancelling stops future charges but does not trigger a refund for the current billing period. You will continue to have access to your subscription benefits until the end of the period you paid for.

To cancel your subscription, contact us at support@sugarme.in or use the cancellation option in your account settings (coming soon).

5. Auto-Renewal

Monthly and yearly subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period. You will be charged the same amount unless you cancel before the renewal date. We recommend cancelling at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged.

We do not send renewal reminders, so please keep track of your billing dates.

6. Payment Disputes

If you believe you have been incorrectly charged, please contact us at support@sugarme.in before initiating a chargeback with your bank. We will work with you to resolve the issue quickly. Initiating an unjustified chargeback may result in account suspension.

7. Contact Us

For any refund-related queries, reach us at:

  • Email: support@sugarme.in
  • Response time: within 2 business days
